The formal moat — Invariant Arithmetic & the Periodic Table

The hardest thing in this portfolio to copy is the theory of measurement underneath it — and it is now running code, not a whitepaper

The Periodic Table of Agent Invariants (v0.5)

A census of 43 named invariants across 10 families — topological (κ cyclicity, β persistence), spatial (σ), temporal (π/ι/ψ), governance (authority-path, deny-by-default, append-only audit), plus deliberation, evaluation, attention, security, embodiment, and federation. Each element is a measurement that tells an agent what position it is in and what authority that position grants. Six already carry machine-checked proofs; κ alone is verified on 1,926,351 finite systems, zero counterexamples.

Invariant Arithmetic v0.3 (the algebra)

The chemistry over those elements: 4 invariant families, 6 operations (combine, chain, promote, reconcile, deliberate, consume) and 15 algebraic laws verified by property-based tests at 1,000 trials each (all pass in ~134 ms). Ships as an Elixir reference plus a framework-free ~400-LOC TypeScript port.

Why this is a moat, not a curiosity: As of the v0.3.0-alpha Workbench release (2026-05-27), Invariant Arithmetic is the live verifier substrate of the Proof Harness. All six proof gates — content-hash, trace-completeness, no-hidden-capability, authority, redaction, replay-fidelity — compute their verdict through a single InvariantArithmetic.consume(value, requirements) call and project onto an invariant family, carrying law + invariant_family annotations. The same JSON file is the user-valuable SkillBundle and the PRISM-facing EvidenceBundle, self-describing as IA-derived. The 2026 research frontier (neuro-symbolic continual learning — NeSyC, trainable graph memory, ALMA) is converging on exactly this idea: a symbolic, verifiable layer over the neural substrate. [&] is the only one shipping it as a property-tested algebra wired into a working verifier.

The thesis for H2 2026 is conversion, not construction. The protocol layer is overbuilt (~90%) relative to implementation (~55%) and go-to-market (~0%). Three forces define the back half of the year, and the [&] stack is positioned at the intersection of all three: (1) the shift from cloud chat assistants to embodied, computer-using agents (the &body / OS-011 bet); (2) the hardening of memory + continual learning from research curiosity into a procurement checkbox (Graphonomous); and (3) the governance and provenance reckoning as agents gain the ability to act (Delegatic + the invariant stack). The roadmap committed to these bets before they were consensus.
